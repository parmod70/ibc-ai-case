The stated opportunity

Supplier contracts, pricing sheets and compliance documents are fragmented, making it slower to access trusted deal terms.

Working hypothesis to validate: Deal information may be spread across supplier contracts, pricing sheets, compliance documents and portal records, making updates slower and harder to govern.
